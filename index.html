Intended learners

Given direction
AI-assisted, adaptive storytelling for digital citizenship learning. The brief is not an unrestricted app assignment.
Current title

Course structure
A year-long COMP2019 software project, with development, testing, reports, and a final showcase.

Open or awaiting clarification

The actual product
Your group develops a focused design within the brief and agrees requirements with the supervisor/client. Exact features are not settled.
What NLP should analyse
The minutes describe emotions in user-written narratives; the brief emphasises learners’ reasoning. These need to be reconciled before choosing an approach.
Learning need and success
Why Foundation students need this tool, how it fits teaching, and how improvement will be measured are not established in the supplied records.
Learning scope and approach
Which digital citizenship elements to cover remains open. Cognitivism is recommended by the supervisor, not recorded as a final team decision.
Implementation and naming
AI model, API, platform, and implementation choices remain open. Discuss a rename or a substantially different idea with the supervisor.
