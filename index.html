Project description

DigiTales AI introduces an NLP-driven adaptive storytelling approach to digital citizenship learning, where learners’ open-ended reasoning is analysed to dynamically personalise narrative pathways, feedback and reflective learning experiences. The platform responds to how and why learners make decisions, using indicators such as empathy, risk perception, self-regulation, and critical judgement to personalise narratives, feedback, and reflection. It further translates learners’ demonstrated understanding into personalised, real-world project challenges, enabling them to apply digital citizenship competencies such as critical evaluation, ethical decision-making, online safety and responsible AI use in authentic contexts.

It also incorporates culturally responsive and multilingual storytelling, allowing scenarios and narratives to be adapted to different cultural, linguistic and social contexts, making digital citizenship learning more inclusive, relevant and meaningful for diverse learners.
